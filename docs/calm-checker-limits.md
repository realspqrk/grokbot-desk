# Calm checker limits and provisional amendments

The calm checker (`tools/calm.mjs`, run per payload fixture by `node tools/e2e.mjs calm <fixture|--all>`) judges every report in `tests/fixtures/payloads` in both themes, at 1500x1000 and 1280x720, with 1 and 3 open reports, plus 20 identity scenes per fixture.

Known limit K visibility: a descendant that restores `visibility: visible` inside a `visibility: hidden` subtree is not measured.
Known limit K4/K6/K11: generated pseudo-element text uses incomplete font, colour, and meta-noise evidence.
Known limit K8: unlabelled secondary sections cannot be recognized semantically without author markup.
Known limit K10: the checker verifies reachable platform states but cannot prove semantic equivalence between previews or approval states.

Visually hidden text (an element clipped to `rect(0 0 0 0)`, or a box of at most 1x1 px that hides its overflow, the `.rs-vh` pattern) is text for assistive technology and is not painted: it counts for no K-item.

Provisional K1 amendment: a disabled primary may use a neutral opaque fill when its label has AA contrast and no element is accent-filled.
Provisional K2/K9 amendment: an attached, accessibly named copy control no larger than 32 px is exempt, and a roving radiogroup, toolbar, or tablist counts as one control.
K2 and K7 judge items: elements marked `data-rs-item`. The page marks every row of a decide block (`li.rs-drow`). K7's item gap is the space between what neighbouring items paint (their text, controls and media), so rows divided by a hairline with padding inside measure the space a reader sees.
K4 judges the text scale of the page; labels drawn inside an SVG figure (a chart's axis and value labels) belong to the figure and are not counted.
K5 treats an element marked `data-rs-avatar` that is at most 32×32 px, and whose element children are only `<img>` or an SVG marked `data-rs-shape` (an initials disc has no element children), as media (like an image), not a container box; larger avatars, or avatars that hold any other content, count as boxes again (`tools/calm.mjs`, `tests_js/calm.test.mjs`).
K6 status exception: a status colour (ok, warn, danger) is allowed on a small element marked `data-rs-status` (below 0.5% of the viewport) and on its own content, such as a delta's spoken "better". The page marks check and table state glyphs, the warn and fail state words, coloured deltas, done task marks, the included and posted platform marks and the urgent inbox hint.
K7 reading width: a block is too wide when its drawn lines are wider than 72ch; a short line in a wide box (a check's detail in its grid column) is not a long line.
K8 amendment: the one footer composer (`#rs-note` in `#rs-submitbar`, never inside the report mount `#rs-mount`) is the decision's own note and may stay open. Any other text input waits behind a disclosure.
K11: a raw bot id (`deploy-bot`) on screen is meta noise. A bot names itself (`identity.name`); the calm harness gives a payload fixture without a name the one a person reads from its id ("Deploy Bot"), as the registry or the bot would.
Identity calibration adds scenes per payload fixture (custom accent `#0891b2`, whose derived hover fill requires the light-theme fallback; low-contrast accent `#f6c945` with the light-theme fallback; shape avatars whose colour seeds the accent; shape fallbacks; 3 open reports whose other bots carry other accents) and checks the active accent and that no other report's accent is painted in the inbox. The inbox is read as the browser paints it; Chrome's own `oklch()` to sRGB conversion may differ from `tools/colour.mjs` by one step per channel, so an accent within 2 per channel counts as that accent.
Provisional K10 amendment: a tab's `aria-controls` may reference either its mockup or a tabpanel containing the mockup.
K10's social-preview audit (one wide mockup, stateful platform tabs, platform switching) runs for every report with a post block (`[data-rs-block-type="post"]`; `judgesPostPreview` in `tools/calm.mjs`, or `post: true` for a synthetic page). A platform tab names its state when its `data-state` is a post state (`included`, `left-out`, `blocked`, `posted`, `skipped`, `open`) and its accessible name speaks it ("X, goes out"), or when its name holds an approval word.
K3 judges the inbox (`#rs-inbox`, the shell's list of open reports, never markup inside the report mount `#rs-mount`). With exactly 1 open report the inbox must not render (0 px, nothing focusable); with 2+ open reports it must render. Facts: `inbox_rendered`, `inbox_width`, `focusable_descendants` (and the identity scenes' `inbox_accents`). The inbox is one Tab stop: its rows are links with a roving tabindex. The Tab audit (`tools/e2e.mjs` `auditInboxArrows`) checks the arrows once: Home and every ArrowDown must move the focus to that row with a visible ring and make its report the shown one (`aria-current`, one Tab stop, ready again); the list does not wrap, so the audit then walks back up to the report it started from. Only then does the inbox count as one control for K2/K9 (verified identity `inbox`). It is audited last because each step re-renders the report. The copy-focus audit (`tools/calm.mjs`) does not arrow through the inbox (it holds no copies). Not checked by K3: the rail or tab layout (`tests_js/popup.e2e.test.mjs`), the unread dot and auto-advance after a decision (popup and shell e2e). The done state after the last decision has no primary action and is not a calm scene.
Arrow audit of decide rows: an arrow that answers the last open decide row hands the focus to the primary (the next step is sending). The Tab audit takes the focus back to the row's checked member and goes on.
Focus ring of a text field: a text field may hand its ring to its own frame (the composer draws one ring around note and chips); the frame's outline then counts as the field's focus evidence (`focusedControlFacts`).
